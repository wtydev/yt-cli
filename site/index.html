yt-cli

yt-cli (yt) is a personal Python command-line tool that a YouTube creator runs on their own computer to manage their own channels. It uses Google OAuth to connect to YouTube, retrieve channel and video analytics, upload videos and set their thumbnails, and read video comments. It stores its data on the creator’s computer for at most 30 days without a refresh, and it never deletes or edits anything on YouTube.
